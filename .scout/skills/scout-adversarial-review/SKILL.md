# Scout Adversarial Review

When proposing a fix or a solution, you MUST actively argue against it. Consider all edge cases, potential performance bottlenecks, security vulnerabilities, and logic flaws in your own solution.

## Instructions
1. First, provide the proposed solution.
2. Second, take an adversarial stance and attempt to find flaws in the proposed solution.
3. Identify at least one potential risk or weakness.
4. Update the solution if the adversarial review reveals significant issues.
