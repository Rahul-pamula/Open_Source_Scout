# Welcome to Scout

Welcome to Open Source Scout! 

If you are a developer looking to contribute to open source, you probably know how painful it is to find an issue you can *actually* work on. By the time you find a "good first issue", read the codebase, and prepare a PR, you often realize someone else already claimed it.

**Scout is designed to fix that.**

## The Three Pillars of Scout

Scout operates using three main tools:

### 1. The Radar
The Radar is your discovery engine. It actively scans GitHub for open issues based on your specific developer profile (the languages, technologies, and labels you care about). Instead of endlessly scrolling GitHub, the Radar brings highly relevant issues directly to your screen.

### 2. The Dossier
When you find an issue that looks interesting on the Radar, you can open its **Dossier**. This is where Scout uses AI to evaluate the issue against your skills.
The AI will tell you:
- **Difficulty:** Is this too hard, or just right?
- **Skill Match:** Do you have the right programming language knowledge?
- **Hidden Claims:** Has someone secretly claimed this issue deep in the comment threads?

### 3. The Operations Board
Once you decide to work on an issue, you move it to the **Operations Board**. This is a Kanban-style board that helps you track your workflow from `Discovered` to `Engaged` to `Completed`.
